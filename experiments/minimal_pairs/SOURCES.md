# Sources and reuse

All 24 situations, fictional service rules and associated 48 variants were newly AI-authored. No customer records or real commercial terms were used. Every rule is explicitly supplied and describes an invented closed test world, not an actual financial product, legal obligation or insurance coverage.

The exact action/determination schema and English option IDs are reused from the earlier clarification72 diagnostic. Generic conjunction, threshold, absent-versus-unknown, equal-source conflict and ambiguous-target patterns are also reused. Tokenizer verification, reference-runtime copying, RAM guarding and the frozen-then-score workflow follow that methodological predecessor. This is a separate deliberately constructed paired diagnostic, not an independent expert holdout or a reason to combine prior suite percentages.

Runtime: [official Cloudflare/clef-flash revision](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef-flash/tree/17f0b0ad64efb65d273590632833508766b2aae6). Apache-2.0 license and original source references, exact dependency versions and full model-file hashes accompany the experiment. Model weights are not redistributed. CPU NF4 execution is experimental and distinct from unquantized/GPU vendor execution.
