# Sources and provenance

All scenarios, thresholds, service terms, fictional product names and gold labels were newly AI-authored for this diagnostic. No customer records or commercial terms were copied. These invented rules are supplied explicitly in every request; they are not assertions about any real bank, insurer, investment provider, law or coverage. No real account action or advice is evaluated.

Primary runtime source: [Cloudflare/clef-flash pinned official release](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef-flash/tree/17f0b0ad64efb65d273590632833508766b2aae6). The official encoder and schema model source are retained in reference_runtime with model-file SHA-256 hashes, package versions and the Apache 2.0 license. Weights are not redistributed.

Methodological predecessor: the custom German bank-support pilot dated 2026-10-02. This follow-on uses the same pinned experimental CPU NF4 runtime and freeze/separate-gold discipline, but new questions, schema and cases. Its percentages must not be pooled with or treated as directly comparable to earlier bank-support, finance or insurance suites.
