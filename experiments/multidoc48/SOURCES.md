# Sources and reuse

The 48 fictional German multi-document cases are newly AI-authored for this stage using sixteen structural templates instantiated across banking, insurance and finance. Twelve domain×stratum families and cross-domain template reuse make these deliberately related cases, not a representative independently sampled holdout. No real customer information, genuine commercial terms, legal rules or previous model predictions were used to write or tune the cases.

The new source/determination schema distinguishes unique source choice from definite outcome. The determination labels and general explicit-rule/threshold/uncertainty methods follow the earlier clarification and minimal-change diagnostics. Tokenizer-cap checks, original native runtime preservation, scoring conventions, freeze workflow and independent result checking reuse methodological infrastructure; no prior benchmark score is pooled or represented as new evidence.

Model and source: [official Cloudflare/clef-flash revision](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef-flash/tree/17f0b0ad64efb65d273590632833508766b2aae6). Apache-2.0 license, dependency versions and model/source hashes accompany the experiment. Weights are not redistributed. Experimental CPU NF4 is distinct from unquantized or GPU vendor execution.
