# Topic inspiration and source boundaries

Retrieved 2026-10-02. These primary bank/regulator pages were used only to identify familiar service topics. All 80 customer messages were newly authored; none are real support records or copied customer messages. No source determines a gold label: all routing, priority and follow-up labels follow the explicitly fictional policy in `data/policy.json`.

- [Sparkasse: Karte sperren](https://www.sparkasse.de/pk/produkte/konten-und-karten/karten/kartenservices/karte-sperren.html): lost/stolen cards, replacement, digital cards and contact with a blocking service informed the card/security topics. Bank-specific workflow details, phone numbers and prices are not asserted by this benchmark.
- [Sparkasse: Karte entsperren](https://www.sparkasse.de/pk/produkte/konten-und-karten/karten/kartenservices/karte-entsperren.html): technical PIN lockouts and differences from lost-card incidents informed card-support coverage. No promise of unlockability is made.
- [BaFin: Empfängerüberprüfung bei Überweisungen](https://bafin.de/DE/verbraucherinnen-verbraucher/themen-finanzprodukte/konten-zahlungen/zahlungen/ueberweisungen-und-lastschriften/empfaengerueberpruefung/empfaengerueberpruefung_node.html): recipient-name mismatch before authorization informed one transfer scenario. The test neither evaluates legal knowledge nor reproduces the page's liability statements.
- [ING: Überweisungen](https://www.ing.de/hilfe/zahlungsverkehr/ueberweisen/ueberweisungen/): transfer status, standing orders and timing functions informed payment-service topics. No transfer or recovery guarantee is made.
- [ING: Lastschriften](https://www.ing.de/hilfe/zahlungsverkehr/ueberweisen/lastschriften/): viewing mandates, disputes and future collections informed direct-debit topics. No statutory deadline or entitlement is tested.
- [ING: Onlinebanking und Brokerage](https://www.ing.de/hilfe/internetbanking/kundenservice/): account access, authentication devices and common service navigation informed the access/TAN topics. No actual credentials, bank URLs or bank-specific reset process appears in a customer message.
- [ING: Produktinformation Girokonto](https://www.ing.de/dokumente/ing-produktinformation-girokonto/): the product's list of cash, account statement, payment and standing-order services informed the breadth of categories. No eligibility, credit or investment questions are tested.
- [comdirect: Adresse ändern](https://www.comdirect.de/faq/adressaenderung?CIF_Check=true): safe contact-data update routes informed the general address-change question. No particular bank's accepted submission channel is assumed.
- [comdirect: FAQ Konto/Depot](https://www.comdirect.de/cms/faq-konto-depot-eroeffnung.html): existence of common price/service questions informed fee and document topics. Current product prices and conditions are deliberately absent.

## Exact limits

This is an 80-case, German-language, purposively stratified synthetic workflow test, not a sample of incoming bank traffic. The counts of topics, linguistic variations and urgency classes were chosen for coverage, not measured prevalence. The source pages provide no evidence that these proportions represent any bank's support volume. There are no actual customers, genuine identifiers, credentials, account balances or financial decisions. All three answers are bounded classifications, not generated advice, completed transactions, verified resolutions or compliance decisions. The policy is an invented test protocol, not the internal policy of any cited bank. Banking-law correctness, consumer rights, compensation, identity verification, authentication security, demographic fairness, production readiness and live fraud detection are not evaluated.
